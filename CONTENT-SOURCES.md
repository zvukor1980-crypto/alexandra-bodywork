## Structure and editing

Static pages in Russian and English are generated by `python tools/build.py`. Edit both language strings there and regenerate. `style.css` contains the shared responsive design; `app.js` only updates the copyright year. All sound controls and audio playback were removed at the user’s request. No dependencies or build step are needed for deployment.
